Our Services Include:

Food Parcel Distribution

With regards to our food parcel distribution service, our organisation provides carefully prepared food packages filled with fresh fruit, bread, canned goods, and other essentials to families in need. As shown in the image, each box is thoughtfully packed with nutritious items like bananas, oranges, tomatoes, and staple groceries—ensuring that vulnerable households receive both immediate relief and balanced nourishment. These packages not only fight hunger but also bring dignity and hope to communities across South Africa.

Water Purification Project

Access to clean water is a fundamental human right, yet many communities in Gauteng face this challenge daily. To respond, Community Food Banks expanded its work in 2024 with a dedicated Water Project aimed at supporting water‑scarce areas. Alongside maintaining and installing community boreholes, we introduced a water tanker program that has already delivered millions of litres of safe water to regions suffering severe shortages. This ongoing initiative has become a lifeline for families, schools, and healthcare facilities, reinforcing our commitment to sustainable, dignity‑focused community support.
