Who Are We

Community Food Banks emerged during South Africa's 2020 COVID-19 lockdown, born out of urgent necessity. What started as a grassroots effort by volunteers—men, women, and children alike—quickly grew into a united movement driven by the need to support vulnerable communities. At the peak of the lockdown, the organisation was delivering up to 12,000 meals daily for three months across sites in Gauteng.

Since then, Community Food Banks has transformed into a collaborative network, partnering with NPOs, CBOs, government bodies, and corporate allies to uplift disadvantaged communities. Central to its mission is tackling South Africa's high unemployment rate by creating opportunities that restore dignity and foster independence.

Guided by the belief that no one should face hunger—whether for food, education, opportunity, or kindness—Community Food Banks continues to empower individuals and communities to thrive. Explore our services or see how you can volunteer or sponsor.

What Guides Us

We are committed to tackling hunger, poverty, and inequality through sustainable, community‑driven initiatives. Guided by compassion, collaboration, and social justice, we strive to empower individuals and families to thrive.

We envision a Gauteng where every individual has access to sufficient, nutritious food and the opportunity to thrive. Through our unwavering commitment to social responsibility, we strive to create a society free from hunger and malnutrition, where communities are empowered to achieve self-sufficiency and sustainable development. Our vision is to foster a culture of empathy, solidarity, and shared prosperity, ensuring that no one is left behind in the pursuit of a brighter future.

  • Collaboration – we work hand‑in‑hand with communities, partners and volunteers.
  • Sustainability – our impact should be lasting, not temporary.
  • Social justice – everyone deserves dignity, food and clean water.
  • Compassion and integrity – we serve with empathy and honesty.
